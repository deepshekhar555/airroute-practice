How it works. Hourly PM2.5 comes from the Open-Meteo air quality forecast (a CAMS model), fetched for your start and end points. For each departure hour it averages the pollution you would meet at the start and the end of the trip, then multiplies by travel time and a breathing-and-shelter factor for your mode. The factors (walk 1.0, cycle 1.8, bus or auto 0.9, metro 0.6, car with windows up 0.55) are planning assumptions, not measurements. Distance is straight-line times 1.3. The AQI bands are CPCB PM2.5 sub-index bands. This is guidance for planning, not medical advice, and it is a model forecast rather than a station reading.
